## Why 9 Dimensions (`[x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, wx, wy, wz]`)? {{ tag: 'guide' }}

A simple 6D projectile model (`position + velocity`) cannot explain padel rallies, because **ball spin dramatically alters both flight and rebounds**:

1. **Position `(x, y, z)` (`3D`, meters):** Where the center of the ball is located inside the [`Court`](/court) coordinate system.
2. **Linear velocity `(vx, vy, vz)` (`3D`, `m/s`):** How fast and in what direction the ball translates. Note that relative airspeed is `v_rel = v - wind_velocity`.
3. **Angular velocity `(wx, wy, wz)` (`3D`, `rad/s`):** How fast the ball spins around each axis using the standard right-hand rule:
   - **Topspin** (`topspin_rpm > 0` in [`BallState.from_shot()`](/ball-state#from-shot)) creates downward Magnus force (`-Z`), making fast drives dip inside the court and jump forward when gripping the turf (`ROLLING`).
   - **Backspin / Slice** (`topspin_rpm < 0`) creates upward Magnus lift (`+Z`), keeping a *bandeja* or *chiquita* floating longer and causing a low, skidding rebound off the glass (`SLIDING`).
   - **Sidespin** (`sidespin_rpm != 0` around `+Z`) curves lateral flight and kicks sideways off back and corner glass walls (*víbora*).

## Forces in Free Flight (RK4 Integration) {{ tag: 'guide' }}

[`BallPhysicsEngine`](/ball-physics-engine) integrates Newton–Euler equations via **4th-order Runge–Kutta (`RK4`)** at small time steps (default `dt = 1/240 s`) combining four effects:

| Physical Term | Formula Implemented in PadelKit | Intuitive Effect on Ball Flight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gravity (`F_g`)** | `(0, 0, -m * g)` (`g = 9.81 m/s²` default) | Pulls the ball downward at constant acceleration. |
| **Aerodynamic Drag (`F_drag`)** | `-0.5 * rho * A * C_D * ||v_rel||² * v_hat`, with `C_D = 0.50 + 0.15 * fuzz_factor` | Slows the ball quadratically with speed; worn, fluffed felt (`fuzz_factor -> 1.0`) increases `C_D` from `0.50` to `0.65`, while high-altitude venues (`rho` lower in [`EnvironmentConditions`](/environment-conditions)) experience less air resistance. |
| **Magnus Lift (`F_magnus`)** | `0.5 * rho * A * C_L * ||v_rel||² * (omega_hat × v_hat)`, using the Spindt model `C_L = 1 / (2.32 + 0.4 / (S + 0.1))` where `S = R * ||omega|| / ||v_rel||` | Curves flight perpendicular to both spin axis and velocity (`0` lift when spin is zero). |
| **Spin Decay (`alpha_ang`)** | `-spin_decay_rate * omega` (`0.001 s⁻¹` default) | Gradually damps spin due to air viscosity during long lobs. |

## Rebound Physics: Sliding vs. Rolling vs. Damped {{ tag: 'guide' }}

When the sphere center approaches a physical surface within ball radius `R` (turf floor, back glass/wall, stepped/uniform side walls, wire mesh fence, open vs closed lateral doorways, optional indoor ceiling, or the `0.88–0.92 m` catenary net), the engine evaluates the contact slip velocity at the point touching the wall:

`v_slip = v_t + (omega × (-R * n))`

Comparing the impulse needed to achieve pure rolling grip against the maximum Coulomb friction impulse (`J_max = mu * |J_n|`) divides impacts into three regimes ([`ContactRegime`](/physics-enums#contact-regime)):

| Contact Regime | Condition | Surface Example | Physical Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`ContactRegime.SLIDING`** | Tangential slip exceeds max Coulomb friction (`||J_roll|| > mu * |J_n|`) | Tempered glass (`glass_friction=0.25`) | Ball slides throughout contact; rebounds faster along the wall while preserving slice/backspin. |
| **`ContactRegime.ROLLING`** | Surface friction grips the ball cleanly before separation | Concrete wall (`wall_friction=0.70`) or turf (`floor_friction=0.45`) | Ball leaves rolling (`v_t' + omega' × (-R*n) = 0`), turning spin directly into forward/upward kick or checking short on heavy backspin. |
| **`ContactRegime.DAMPED`** | Impact against flexible tensioned mesh | Center net (`net_damping=0.30`) | Velocity is damped smoothly without full elastic specular reflection. |

Notice also two padel-specific court boundary details built directly into [`BallPhysicsEngine`](/ball-physics-engine):
- **Doorways vs. Overhead Mesh Lintels:** If `door_left` or `door_right` is open and the ball passes through `access_left` / `access_right` below `access.height`, it **exits the court without bouncing**. Above `access.height` (or when `DoorState.CLOSED`), it rebounds off [`SurfaceType.SIDE_FENCE`](/physics-enums#surface-type).
- **Randomness on Wire Fence:** [`SurfaceMaterials.fence_randomness`](/surface-materials) optionally perturbs the surface normal vector (`rng` seeded in [`simulate()`](/ball-physics-engine#simulate)) to emulate irregular rebounds off diamond metallic mesh.

    Use [`BallState.from_shot()`](/ball-state#from-shot) to launch a ball using human-friendly `topspin_rpm` and `sidespin_rpm`, run [`engine.simulate()`](/ball-physics-engine#simulate), inspect every [`BounceEvent`](/bounce-event), and convert the resulting [`BallTrajectory`](/ball-trajectory) into a DataFrame or 3D [`MatchTimeline`](/match-timeline).

    <CodeGroup title="Simulate a Cross-Court Bandeja" tag="guide" label="BallPhysicsEngine">

    ```python
    from padelkit import BallPhysicsEngine, BallState

    engine = BallPhysicsEngine()

    # Cross-court bandeja: ~59 km/h with 1,800 RPM backspin + 400 RPM sidespin
    shot = BallState.from_shot(
        position=(-2.5, -2.0, 2.3),
        velocity=(3.5, 16.0, -2.5),
        topspin_rpm=-1800.0,
        sidespin_rpm=400.0,
    )

    traj = engine.simulate(shot, duration_s=2.5, dt=1 / 240, seed=42)

    for b in traj.bounces:
        print(f"t={b.timestamp:.2f}s | {b.surface.value} | {b.regime.value}")

    # Convert directly to Pandas DataFrame or 3D visualizer timeline
    df = traj.to_dataframe()
    timeline = traj.to_timeline(fps=60.0)
    ```

## API Reference

- [`BallPhysicsEngine`](/ball-physics-engine) — 9D RK4 integrator, `step()`, `simulate()`, and vectorized NumPy `simulate_batch()`.
- [`BallState`](/ball-state) — Instantaneous 9D state dataclass (`from_shot`, `from_vector`, `speed_kmh`, `spin_rpm`).
- [`BallTrajectory`](/ball-trajectory) — Time-series trajectory arrays, `state_at()`, analytics (`max_height`, `max_speed`), CSV/DataFrame & `to_timeline()`.
- [`BounceEvent`](/bounce-event) — Recorded pre/post-impact velocity, spin, surface, and contact regime snapshot.
- [`BallProperties`](/ball-properties) — Mass, radius, moment of inertia, felt `fuzz_factor`, pressure, FIP colors, and presets (`worn`, `high_altitude`, `wet`).
- [`EnvironmentConditions`](/environment-conditions) — Gravity, air density, wind, spin decay, and Tetens barometric `from_atmosphere()`.
- [`SurfaceMaterials`](/surface-materials) — Restitution (`cor`) and friction across glass, masonry, floor, wire fence, and net.
- [Physics Enums](/physics-enums) — `BallColor`, `SurfaceType`, `ContactRegime`.
