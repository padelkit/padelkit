# Stroke & Skeletal Animation Library

PadelKit bridges **9D ball aerodynamics** ([`BallState.from_shot()`](/ball-state#from-shot)) with **3D skeletal animations** (both `.glb` Mixamo clips from `padelkit-animator` and baked 17-joint H36M poses inside [`MatchTimeline`](/match-timeline)) via a canonical stroke taxonomy and impact-phase synchronization contract (`contact_swing_phase`). {{ className: 'lead' }}

<Note>
  Looking for an interactive 3D coaching view? Explore the **[PadelKit 3D Stroke Lab](https://padelkit.dev/strokes)** to orbit around the player, scrub frame-by-frame through preparation, impact, and 9D glass rebounds, and inspect live biomechanical cues.
</Note>

## Stroke Taxonomy & Physics Signatures {{ tag: 'guide' }}

Every action in a simulated or reconstructed rally maps to an animation label on [`PlayerPose.animation`](/player-pose) and a corresponding physical spin/velocity signature on launch:

| Animation ID (`PlayerPose.animation`) | Shot Family | Typical Launch & Height | Spin Profile (`topspin_rpm`, `sidespin_rpm`) & Tactical Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`serve`** | Underhand Slice Serve | Struck below waist (`z ≈ 0.85–0.98 m`) after one bounce | Carved backspin (`-600` to `-1200 RPM`) + sidespin (`+300` to `+500 RPM`) to skid low off glass (`SLIDING` regime). |
| **`shot_forehand`** | Forehand Drive / *Bajada* | Struck at `z ≈ 0.75–1.10 m` | Flat or topspin (`+800` to `+1800 RPM`) producing a dipping arc and forward bounce (`ROLLING` transition). |
| **`shot_backhand`** | Backhand / Slice | Left-side drive or defensive glass exit | Topspin (`+500` to `+1400 RPM`) for passing shots, or backspin (`-900 RPM`) for low *chiquitas*. |
| **`shot_volley`** | Net Volley | Compact punch near net (`y ≈ ±2.5–3.8 m`) | Crisp underspin (`-800` to `-1500 RPM`) so the ball stays low into the corners. |
| **`shot_bandeja`** | *Bandeja* (Defensive Overhead) | Shoulder-to-head height (`z ≈ 1.75–2.10 m`) | Open face + heavy backspin (`-1800` to `-2500 RPM`) to hold net position with a slow rebound off back glass. |
| **`shot_vibora`** | *Víbora* (Side-Spin Overhead) | Whipped lateral overhead | Underspin (`-1200 RPM`) + strong sidespin (`±1800` to `±2600 RPM`) that kicks sideways out of the corner glass. |
| **`shot_lob`** | Tactical Lob | High arc (`vz ≈ 8.5–10.5 m/s`, apex `z > 5.5 m`) | Neutral/light spin from the backcourt to force net players behind the service line without flying straight into back glass. |
| **`shot_smash`** | Smash (*Remate* / *Por 3* / *Por 4*) | High-point overhead (`z ≈ 2.35–2.75 m`) | Flat power (`> 130 km/h`) to bring back over the net, or heavy topspin (`+2600` to `+3400 RPM`) to kick out over `3.0 m` side mesh. |

## Impact-Phase Synchronization (`contact_swing_phase`) {{ tag: 'guide' }}

<Row>
  <Col>

    When simulating a rally in [`MatchTimeline`](/match-timeline), the physics engine records the exact timestamp `t_hit` at which the ball leaves the racket strings. If a skeletal clip merely started at `t_hit`, the player would swing *after* the ball was already flying away.

    To synchronize racket impact with physics ball launch intuitively:
    1. Each `.glb` entry in `manifest.json` stores `contact_swing_phase` (`phi_c = f_contact / (N_frames - 1)` in `[0, 1]`).
    2. During **preparation / backswing** (`t <= t_hit`, where [`PlayerPose.swing_phase`](/player-pose) rises `0 -> 1`), [`PadelVisualizer`](/padel-visualizer) scrubs the clip from `0` to `phi_c * T_clip`.
    3. At **impact (`t = t_hit`, `swing_phase == 1.0`)**, the hitting wrist/hand (`mixamorig:RightHand` or joint `16: RightWrist` in [`H36M_JOINT_NAMES`](/viz-constants#h36-m-joint-names)) meets the ball exactly at `f_contact`.
    4. During **follow-through** (`t > t_hit`, `swing_phase` decays `1 -> 0`), playback continues smoothly from `phi_c * T_clip` to the end of the clip.

  </Col>
