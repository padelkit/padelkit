# Padel Stroke & Skeletal Animation Library

PadelKit unifies **9D ball aerodynamics** (`BallState.from_shot`) with **3D Mixamo skeletal animations** (`padelkit-animator` `.glb` assets) through a standardized stroke taxonomy and impact-phase synchronization contract (`contact_swing_phase`). {{ className: 'lead' }}

<Note>
  Looking for an interactive 3D coaching view? Explore the **[PadelKit 3D Stroke Lab](https://padelkit.dev/strokes)** to orbit around the player, scrub frame-by-frame through preparation, impact, and 9D glass rebounds, and inspect live biomechanical cues.
</Note>

## Stroke Taxonomy & Physics Reference

Every stroke in a simulated or reconstructed padel rally maps to a canonical `padelkit_animation_id` on `PlayerPose.animation` and a physical launch signature (`velocity`, `topspin_rpm`, `sidespin_rpm`) in `BallState.from_shot()`.

<Properties>
  <Property name="serve" type="SERVE · Underhand Slice">
    Executed below waist height (`z ≈ 0.85–0.98 m`) after a single floor bounce in the server's box. Typically imparted with carved backspin (`-600` to `-1200 RPM`) and sidespin (`+300` to `+500 RPM`) so the ball stays low (`SLIDING` regime) after rebounding off the receiver's side or back glass.
  </Property>
  <Property name="shot_forehand" type="FOREHAND · Groundstroke & Wall Exit">
    Standard right-side drive or *bajada de pared* struck at `z ≈ 0.75–1.10 m`. Flat or topspin (`+800` to `+1800 RPM`) produces a dipping parabola over the net that kicks forward upon floor contact (`ROLLING` transition).
  </Property>
  <Property name="shot_backhand" type="BACKHAND · Groundstroke & Defensive Slice">
    Left-side groundstroke or defensive backhand slice off the back glass. Uses either flat/topspin (`+500` to `+1400 RPM`) for passing drives or backspin (`-900 RPM`) for low cross-court chiquitas to the opponent's feet.
  </Property>
  <Property name="shot_volley" type="VOLLEY · Net Intercept">
    Compact punch stroke executed close to the net (`y ≈ ±2.5–3.8 m`) with minimal backswing and crisp underspin (`-800` to `-1500 RPM`) to keep the ball skidding deep into the corners.
  </Property>
  <Property name="shot_bandeja" type="BANDEJA · Defensive/Tactical Overhead">
    Signature padel overhead struck at shoulder-to-head height (`z ≈ 1.75–2.10 m`) with a flat-to-open racket face and heavy backspin (`-1800` to `-2500 RPM`). Designed to retain net position by producing a low, slow rebound off the back glass.
  </Property>
  <Property name="shot_vibora" type="VIBORA · Aggressive Side-Spin Overhead">
    Whipped lateral overhead struck with combined underspin (`-1200 RPM`) and heavy sidespin (`±1800` to `±2600 RPM`). After hitting the corner glass, the lateral tangential velocity causes the ball to bite sideways away from the defender.
  </Property>
  <Property name="shot_lob" type="LOB · High Tactical Reset">
    High launch angle (`vz ≈ 8.5–10.5 m/s`, apex `z > 5.5 m`) struck from behind the service line to push the net players back to the baseline without hitting the `4.0 m` back glass on the fly.
  </Property>
  <Property name="shot_smash" type="SMASH · Power Flat / Kick X3 / X4">
    High-point overhead (`z ≈ 2.35–2.75 m`) struck with either flat power (`> 130 km/h`) to bring the ball back to the hitter's side or heavy topspin (`+2600` to `+3400 RPM`) so the ball kicks high off the `3.0 m` back glass and exits over the `3.0 m` side fence (*por 3*).
  </Property>
</Properties>

## Impact-Phase Synchronization (`contact_swing_phase`)

<Row>
  <Col>

    When simulating a rally in `MatchTimeline`, the physics engine determines the exact timestamp `t_hit` at which the ball leaves the strings. If a skeletal animation clip were simply triggered at `t_hit`, the player would start their backswing *after* the ball is already in flight.

    To align the 3D racket swing with the physical ball launch:
    1. Each `.glb` entry in `manifest.json` stores `contact_swing_phase` (`phi_c = f_contact / (N_frames - 1)` in `[0, 1]`).
    2. During the **preparation / backswing** window (`t <= t_hit`, where `PlayerPose.swing_phase` rises from `0 -> 1`), `PadelVisualizer` scrubs the `.glb` clip from `0` to `phi_c * T_clip`.
    3. At the **exact impact frame** (`t = t_hit`, `swing_phase == 1.0`), the skeleton's hand bone (`mixamorig:RightHand`) is guaranteed to be at `f_contact`.
    4. During the **follow-through** window (`t > t_hit`, where `swing_phase` decays from `1 -> 0`), the clip continues smoothly from `phi_c * T_clip` to `T_clip`.

  </Col>
