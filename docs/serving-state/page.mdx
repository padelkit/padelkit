# ServingState

The `ServingState` dataclass encapsulates the serving team, player, receiver, and court side for the upcoming/next point in the match. It is exposed through the `server` property of the [`MatchScore`](/match-score). {{ className: 'lead' }}

## Properties

<Properties>
  <Property name="team" type="TeamId">
    The [`TeamId`](/team) about to serve the upcoming/next point (`TeamId.A` or `TeamId.B`).
  </Property>
  <Property name="player" type="Player | None">
    The specific [`Player`](/player) about to serve the upcoming/next point, or `None` if player details are not configured.
  </Property>
  <Property name="receiver" type="Player | None">
    The specific [`Player`](/player) about to receive the upcoming/next serve, or `None` if team details are not configured.
  </Property>
  <Property name="court_side" type="'left' | 'right' | None">
    The side of the court from which the serve is executed (`"left"` or `"right"`), or `None`.
  </Property>
</Properties>
