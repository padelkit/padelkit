## Official FIP Layout & Dimensions {{ tag: 'guide' }}

An official doubles padel court has an **interior playing area of `20.0 m × 10.0 m`** divided across the midpoint by a `10.0 m` net (`0.88 m` high at the center strap and `0.92 m` at the side posts). Each half-court (`10.0 m × 10.0 m`) has a service line placed `3.0 m` in front of its back wall (`6.95 m` from the net, measured inside lines).

All spatial quantities in PadelKit follow **SI meters (`m`)** unless explicitly stated otherwise. See [`CourtDimensions`](/court-dimensions) for all 11 geometric fields and regulatory tolerances.

## Coordinate Systems & Perspective Convention {{ tag: 'guide' }}

A recurring source of confusion in multi-camera sports geometry is deciding where `(0, 0, 0)` sits and what "near", "far", "left", and "right" mean. PadelKit enforces one unambiguous convention across court geometry, cameras, physics, and 3D visualization:

- **Point of view (`NEAR` / `FAR`, `LEFT` / `RIGHT`):** Always oriented from a **player standing on the `NEAR` side facing the net** (matching the standard TV broadcast camera behind the near back glass):
  - **`-X` (Left) to `+X` (Right):** Court width (`10.0 m`). Left sideline is `-X`, right sideline is `+X`.
  - **`-Y` (Near) to `+Y` (Far):** Court length (`20.0 m`). Behind the camera is the **NEAR** half (`Y < 0` in centered coordinates); across the net is the **FAR** half (`Y > 0`).
  - **`+Z` (Up):** Height vertically above the court floor (`Z = 0.0` at the playing surface).

PadelKit exposes two reference frames through [`CoordinateSystem`](/court-enums#coordinate-system):

| Coordinate System | Origin `(0, 0, 0)` | `X` Range (`10 m` width) | `Y` Range (`20 m` length) | Typical Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`CoordinateSystem.CENTERED`** *(default)* | Center of the net on the floor ([`CENTER`](/court-landmark)) | `[-5.0, +5.0]` | `[-10.0, +10.0]` | Physics simulation, Three.js / Blender 3D scenes, symmetrical analytics |
| **`CoordinateSystem.CORNER_BASED`** | Near-left floor corner ([`FLOOR_NEAR_LEFT`](/court-landmark)) | `[0.0, 10.0]` | `[0.0, 20.0]` | 2D top-down court maps and non-negative pixel/grid heatmaps |

## Enclosure Walls, Mesh Heights & Corner Profiles {{ tag: 'guide' }}

Under official FIP regulations (*Cerramientos*), the perimeter of a padel court combines **solid rebound panels** (tempered glass or concrete/masonry wall) with **electro-welded metallic wire mesh**. Every back wall (`10.0 m` wide × `4.0 m` high) always consists of `3.0 m` of solid wall topped by `1.0 m` of metallic mesh.

On the **`20.0 m` sidelines**, [`Court`](/court) models the geometry orthogonally along three independent axes:

1. **Material (`EnclosureType`):** `GLASS` (tempered safety glass, low friction so slice/backspin slides fast) vs `WALL` (brick/concrete wall, higher friction that grips spin). Both materials can be stepped or uniform under FIP rules.
2. **Upper mesh height (`EnclosureVariant`):** In `V1`, the central `16.0 m` wire mesh drops to `3.0 m` (with `4.0 m` only in the `2.0 m` corners adjacent to each back wall). In `V2`, the perimeter stays at a uniform `4.0 m` height all the way down the sideline.
3. **Solid corner silhouette (`SideWallProfile`):** Over the `4.0 m` end zones from each back wall, `STEPPED` places two panels stepping down (`3.0 m × 2.0 m` then `2.0 m × 2.0 m`), whereas `UNIFORM` (*Variant Crystal*, "sin escalón") keeps a flat `3.0 m × 4.0 m` solid glass panel. When omitted (`None`), `WALL` courts default to `STEPPED` and `GLASS` courts default to `UNIFORM`.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Variant 1 (Stepped)** | `WALL` or `GLASS` | `V1` | `STEPPED` | `3.0 m × 2.0 m` + `2.0 m × 2.0 m` | `3.0 m` |

| **Variant 1 Crystal** | `GLASS` | `V1` | `UNIFORM` | Flat `3.0 m × 4.0 m` | `3.0 m` |
| **Variant 2 Crystal** | `GLASS` | `V2` | `UNIFORM` | Flat `3.0 m × 4.0 m` | `4.0 m` |

Both [`BallPhysicsEngine`](/ball-physics-engine) and [`PadelVisualizer`](/padel-visualizer) query [`Court.side_wall_heights_at()`](/court#side-wall-heights-at) and [`Court.side_enclosure_panels()`](/court#side-enclosure-panels) directly so rebound physics and rendered 3D walls never disagree.

    ### Configuring a Stepped vs Crystal Court

    Pass only the regulatory overrides you need when building a [`Court`](/court). For instance, choosing `EnclosureType.WALL` automatically resolves `effective_side_wall_profile` to `SideWallProfile.STEPPED`, while passing `CourtDimensions.fip_variant_2()` automatically selects `EnclosureVariant.V2` (`4.0 m` sides).

  </Col>
  <Col sticky>

    <CodeGroup title="Enclosure Configuration" tag="guide" label="Court">

    ```python
    from padelkit import (
        Court,
        CourtDimensions,
        EnclosureType,
        EnclosureVariant,
        SideWallProfile,
    )

    # 1. FIP Variant 1 masonry court (defaults to STEPPED)
    wall_v1 = Court(
        enclosure_type=EnclosureType.WALL,
        enclosure_variant=EnclosureVariant.V1,
    )

    # 2. FIP Variant 2 Crystal glass court (4m continuous mesh + flat 3x4m glass)
    crystal_v2 = Court(
        dimensions=CourtDimensions.fip_variant_2(),
        enclosure_type=EnclosureType.GLASS,
        enclosure_variant=EnclosureVariant.V2,
        side_wall_profile=SideWallProfile.UNIFORM,
    )

    # Inspect exact (solid_height_m, total_height_m) 3.0 m inward from a back wall
    print("wall_v1 @ 3m:", wall_v1.side_wall_heights_at(3.0))
    print("crystal_v2 @ 3m:", crystal_v2.side_wall_heights_at(3.0))
    # wall_v1 @ 3m: (2.0, 3.0)
    # crystal_v2 @ 3m: (3.0, 4.0)
    ```

## Lateral Doorways, Exterior Safety Zones & Ceilings {{ tag: 'guide' }}

In padel, lateral exits are more than architectural openings: **Rule 16 (Out-of-Court Play)** lets players leave the court through open side doorways to chase smashes (*por 3* / *por 4*). Each sideline in PadelKit therefore combines:

1. **Opening Geometry ([`SideAccess`](/side-access)):** Centered at `Y = 0.0` (the net line), configured independently per side (`access_left`, `access_right`):
   - **Single access (`count=1`):** One central doorway `1.05–2.00 m` wide × `2.00–2.20 m` tall (default `2.00 m × 2.20 m` FIP maximum).
   - **Dual access (`count=2`):** Two symmetric doorways `0.72–1.00 m` wide × `2.00–2.20 m` tall separated by a central structural post at `Y = 0`.
   - **No access (`count=0`):** Continuous wire mesh (`12.0 m` span), common on recreational courts with entry on one side only.
   - **Overhead lintel:** Above `Z > access.height` (e.g. `2.20 m` up to `3.0 m` or `4.0 m`), metallic mesh still exists and rebounds balls normally!
2. **Door Gate State ([`DoorState`](/court-enums#door-state)):** Setting `door_left=DoorState.CLOSED` treats the doorway as closed metallic mesh during physics collisions and renders a closed gate in [`PadelVisualizer`](/padel-visualizer).
3. **Exterior Safety Clearance ([`SafetyZone`](/safety-zone)):** Official out-of-court play requires **both** sidelines to have `count=2` doorways, an obstacle-free exterior clearance zone at least **`2.0 m` wide × `8.0 m` long** (`4.0 m` to each side of the net) × **`3.0 m` high**, and at least **`0.02 m` (`2 cm`)** of shock-absorbing padding on frames and posts.
4. **Surface Color & Indoor Ceiling ([`CourtSurfaceColor`](/court-enums#court-surface-color)):** FIP allows `BLUE`, `GREEN`, and `TERRACOTTA` anywhere, restricts `BLACK` exclusively to `CourtSetting.INDOOR`, and requires `ceiling_height >= 6.0 m` (`8.0 m` recommended for new indoor construction). When `ceiling_height` is set, balls hitting the roof trigger a [`SurfaceType.CEILING`](/physics-enums#surface-type) impact.

| **`SideAccess.no_access()`** | `0` (solid mesh) | Continuous wire mesh | No |

    ### Validating FIP Compliance Programmatically

    Even non-official club courts can be modeled without errors; whenever you need to audit whether a venue meets official FIP rules (including indoor black turf, ceiling clearance, doorways, and Rule 16 safety zones), call [`court.is_fip_compliant()`](/court#fip-regulatory-validation) or inspect exact reasons via [`court.fip_compliance_issues()`](/court#fip-regulatory-validation).

  </Col>
  <Col sticky>

    <CodeGroup title="Doorways, Safety Zones & FIP Audit" tag="guide" label="Court">

    ```python
    from padelkit import (
        Court,
        CourtSetting,
        CourtSurfaceColor,
        DoorState,
        SafetyZone,
        SideAccess,
    )

    # Tournament venue certified for official out-of-court play
    pro_court = Court(
        turf_color=CourtSurfaceColor.BLUE,
        access_left=SideAccess.fip_dual_access(),
        access_right=SideAccess.fip_dual_access(),
        safety_zone_left=SafetyZone.fip_standard(),
        safety_zone_right=SafetyZone.fip_standard(),
    )
    print("Out-of-court allowed:", pro_court.is_out_of_court_play_allowed())
    print("FIP issues:", pro_court.fip_compliance_issues(out_of_court_play=True))

    # Recreational indoor court with single-side entry and closed right side
    club_court = Court(
        access_left=SideAccess.fip_single_access(width=1.6, height=2.1),
        access_right=SideAccess.no_access(),
        door_left=DoorState.OPEN,
        setting=CourtSetting.INDOOR,
        ceiling_height=7.0,
        turf_color=CourtSurfaceColor.BLACK,
    )
    print("Passable through left door @ z=1.4m:", club_court.is_doorway_opening_at("left", 0.0, 1.4))
    print("Passable through lintel @ z=2.5m:", club_court.is_doorway_opening_at("left", 0.0, 2.5))
    ```

## API Reference

- [`Court`](/court) — Main immutable court assembly, landmarks, panel geometry, doorways, and FIP checks.
- [`CourtDimensions`](/court-dimensions) — All 11 official court measurements and regulatory presets (`V1` / `V2` / new construction).
- [`SideAccess`](/side-access) — Lateral doorway configuration (`0`, `1`, or `2` openings) and Y-interval solver.
- [`SafetyZone`](/safety-zone) — Exterior Rule 16 clearance volume and doorway/post padding.
- [`SideEnclosurePanel`](/side-enclosure-panel) & [`SideEnclosureSegment`](/side-enclosure-segment) — Full-length sideline panel decompositions for physics and 3D rendering.
- [`CourtLandmark`](/court-landmark) — Standardized 17-point calibration landmark index (`1–13` floor, `14–17` wall tops).
- [`CourtLocation`](/court-location) — Optional club, city, country, and GPS venue metadata.
- [Court Enums](/court-enums) — `CoordinateSystem`, `EnclosureType`, `EnclosureVariant`, `SideWallProfile`, `CourtSurfaceColor`, `CourtOrientation`, `DoorState`, `CourtSetting`.
